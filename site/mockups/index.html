Velvet theme mockups

One status page, six designs. Every page below loads the same base.css, the same engine, and the same dummy data. The only difference between them is which theme file the second <link> points at.

Themes

  • Velvet today The page Velvet publishes now, expressed as a theme file. The baseline the other six are compared against.
  • Cassette Futurism 1979 Amber phosphor on olive, chamfered corners, one monospaced face throughout.
  • Populuxe 1958 Manila paper, vermilion and petrol, capsule segments, a geometric sans over an italic serif.
  • Vector Grid 1982 Unfilled surfaces, lit edges, a horizon grid, and a five-line graticule behind the chart.
  • Twenty Forty-Nine 2049 A filthy pane of glass with a dim teal readout behind it: corner brackets, edge scales, dotted grids and a dark vignette.
  • NCC-1701-D 2364 A divided column of coloured segments carrying the service names, capsule end-caps, and everything ranged right.

What to try

Switch the range between 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d and 1yr, and watch the strip change resolution: a year is grouped into weeks, so a bar there covers seven days and its tooltip names a span rather than a date. Hover a segment for its reading. Open a service for the protocol readings and the response chart, then hover the chart or focus it and use the arrow keys. The toolbar at the top switches between the two layouts the product supports, except on a theme whose structure has only one.

Where the rules are

documentation/theme-authoring.md lists every token and what it may be set to. documentation/theme-authoring.md is the template for building another one, with the reasoning behind each decision and the variations it rules out.
